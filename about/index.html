About Me

AI & Data Engineer building data platforms and AI-powered products.

I’m Amir Zare, an AI & Data Engineer based in Yerevan, Armenia, and the founder of DataLayer, an AI-powered analytics platform for e-commerce sellers.

Over 7+ years I’ve grown from enterprise backend engineering in PHP and Laravel to modern data platforms — Airflow, dbt, Kafka, Flink, BigQuery — and production AI systems. I design reliable, cost-efficient pipelines that move quickly from prototype to production, and I ship LLM applications with deterministic guardrails.

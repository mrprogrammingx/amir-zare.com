Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who runs this site

amir-zare.com is the personal website of Amir Zare, based in Yerevan, Armenia. For any privacy question or request, email info@amir-zare.com.

What this site collects

The site has no accounts, forms, or newsletter, so it doesn’t ask you for personal information. To understand how the site is used and improve it, every visit is measured with two analytics tools:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC): pages visited, how you arrived (for example a search engine, LinkedIn, or a QR code), approximate location (country and city), device, browser, language, and time on page.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation): how pages are used, such as clicks, scrolling, and mouse movement, shown as heatmaps and anonymized session replays. Clarity masks text you type.

These tools set cookies and use similar technologies in your browser to recognize repeat visits. They don’t receive your name, email address, or phone number from this site. Data is reported in aggregate and is never sold or used for advertising by this site.

Who processes the data

Google and Microsoft process analytics data on their servers, which may be outside your country, including in the United States. Their own policies apply: Google Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

How long it is kept

Google Analytics keeps event data for 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically. Microsoft Clarity keeps session recordings for up to 30 days and aggregated heatmap data for up to 13 months.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or browse in a private window.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Use a content or tracker blocker, which stops both tools from loading.
  • Email info@amir-zare.com to ask about or request deletion of data related to your visits.

Video and contact card

The video on the About page is hosted on YouTube. Nothing from YouTube loads until you press play; it then plays in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and Google’s Privacy Policy applies.

The “Save my contact” button on the contact page gives you Amir’s own contact details. It doesn’t collect yours.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
